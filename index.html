3
What are the different types of games?

CashoN offers various game types:

  • Single - Pick a number between 0-9
  • Jodi - Pick a pair of numbers between 00-99
  • Single Patti - 3-digit number with no repeating digits
  • Double Patti - 3-digit number with one repeating digit
  • Triple Patti - 3-digit number with all same digits
  • Half Sangam - Combination of Single and Patti
  • Full Sangam - Combination of opening and closing Patti
